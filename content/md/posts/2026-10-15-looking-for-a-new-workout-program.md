i am pretty strong for a normie, and pretty weak for consistent lifters. that puts me in an odd spot when looking at new workout programs.


i did [bullmastiff](https://empire-barbell.com/2022/08/09/complete-breakdown-of-bullmastiff-plus-free-pdf-of-the-program/) to great success, hitting PRs in september when i tested my squat (235), bench (185), and deadlift (315). i liked that once i put in my starting weights, it calculated the weight increases for me. but it's a long program and by the end, i was bored of it, especially as i'm getting further into my transition. (2 months on monday :tada:)

i think what i want next is a version of "big glutes save lives", where i focus on my lower body to help give me definition and lessen the work on my upper body so i don't have the masculine v-shape. the program as written is extremely light, built for people who have never worked out before. i'm not a physical therapist or coach and i don't have a theory of muscle groups or how workouts impact the body, which makes me feel like i shouldn't change it willy nilly.

anyone wanna give me a good intermediate lifting program that will make my ass big and also push me?
