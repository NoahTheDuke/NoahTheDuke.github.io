my name is naomi.

at birth, i was gifted the name "noah" by my parents and it served me well for more than 39 years. in 2026, i changed it to "noa" as i began to openly explore my gender and sexuality. and now i have settled on my (hopefully final) name.

### Q/A

> why naomi?

a couple of reasons:

1) i liked my initials and wanted to keep "n".
2) i liked that both "noah" and "naomi" come from stories in the hebrew bible. the story of ruth and naomi is really meaningful to me. loss, love, a binding that goes beyond reason. ruth saying "where you go i will go, where you stay i will stay. your people shall be my people and your god my god. where you die i will die and be buried."
3) i've known many cool naomis, and reading "doppelganger" by naomi klein was magical a couple years ago.

> what other names did you consider?

nadine, neve (naimh), and jessica.

shout out to my mom who wanted to name me jessica if i'd been born with a vulva but i'm too "unique-name"-pilled for that one. neve is beautiful and i have irish ancestry, but while i have redheaded siblings, i'm not meaningfully irish in any way. it feels weird to say "i'll use this unknown english name to dip into a different culture that i'm only tenuously connected with" when i can't even spell the original without looking it up. and "nadine" is fun because it's DW's invisible friend in the pbs show Arthur, but otherwise it doesn't thrill me.

> why not stick with noa?

noa is a beautiful name. however, "noah" is a traditionally male name and even with the rise of "noa" in popularity, i personally can't break away from it as a man's name. i feel like a man when i hear it and when i speak it, no matter how i present myself.

> what about your deadname?

i don't like the "deadname" phrasing (i find it to be unnecessarily harsh and cruel to both my past self and the ways others may think of me) but i will be strict about this and will correct folks who use my old name. my old name refers to that period of my life and it no longer applies, and i ask that all you who know me find strength within yourselves to change how you think of me internally and externally.

> you have been using "noahtheduke" for 20 years and "noahbogart.com" for 3. what are you gonna do about those?

man i don't fuckin know. i've been switching away from "noahtheduke" already, so that process will now point to whatever new handle i choose, and "noahbogart.com" is only for atproto stuff which should hopefully swap over because good apps use the did:plc and not my literal handle lol. but yolo i guess.
